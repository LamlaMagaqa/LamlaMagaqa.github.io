Privacy Policy for Royals by Eve

Effective Date: 01 July 2026

At Royals by Eve, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect the information you provide when using our services, visiting our salon, interacting with our website, or communicating with us through phone, email, social media, or online booking platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal information:

  • Full name
  • Contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Appointment and booking information
  • Service history and preferences
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party providers)
  • Marketing preferences
  • Feedback, reviews, and survey responses
  • Photographs or videos taken with your consent for promotional purposes
  • Information voluntarily provided through forms, consultations, or correspondence

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Provide salon, beauty, wellness, hair, nail, brow, and lash services
  • Process payments and issue invoices
  • Communicate regarding bookings, cancellations, and reminders
  • Improve our services and customer experience
  • Respond to enquiries and customer support requests
  • Send promotional offers, updates, and marketing communications where consent has been provided
  • Maintain internal records and comply with legal obligations

3. Marketing Communications

With your consent, we may send you promotional messages, special offers, product updates, and salon news via email, SMS, WhatsApp, or social media.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking the unsubscribe link in emails
  • Contacting us directly
  • Replying with "STOP" where applicable

4. Sharing of Information

Royals by Eve does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share information with:

  • Payment processing providers
  • Appointment booking systems
  • IT and website service providers
  • Professional advisors where required
  • Government authorities when legally obligated

All third parties are required to protect your information and process it in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

5. Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, disclosure, or alteration.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Photographs and Social Media

From time to time, we may take photographs or videos of completed services for marketing, portfolio, or social media purposes.

No photographs or videos will be published without your prior consent. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information where legally permissible
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications
  • Object to certain forms of processing
  • Lodge a complaint regarding the handling of your personal information

8. Cookies and Website Usage

If you visit our website, cookies and similar technologies may be used to improve user experience, analyse website traffic, and support website functionality.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

9. Compliance with South African Law

Royals by Eve processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) and any other applicable South African privacy legislation.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on our website and become effective upon publication.
